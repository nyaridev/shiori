Write down every decision that would be expensive to undo, newest first. A good entry says what we chose, the
reasons, and the options we turned down, so future us can tell whether the reasons still hold.

| When | Decision | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Keep everything in this wiki | One place to look; agents keep it up to date | :badge[Final]{tone="good"} |
| Week 1 | Ship one core feature first | Small enough for two people to finish | :badge[Final]{tone="good"} |
| Week 1 | Launch on one platform only | Learn faster, spread less thin | :badge[Revisit at launch]{tone="warn"} |

## Example: where to launch

:::compare{pick="A marketplace"}
### Our own website
- Pro: Full control over the look
- Pro: No platform fees
- Con: We bring every visitor ourselves

### A marketplace
- Pro: People already shop there
- Con: A fee on each sale
- Status: :status[active]
:::

## Template

:::details[Copy this for a big decision]
**Decision:** what we chose.

**Context:** what made us decide now.

**Options:**

1. The one we picked, and why.
2. An alternative, and why not.

**Revisit when:** the condition that would change our mind.
:::

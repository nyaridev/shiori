Every idea gets a line here, however small. When one keeps coming back, it earns a page of its own and a place on the
[[Roadmap]].

| Idea | Why it might work | Effort | Excitement |
|---|---|---|---|
| A behind-the-scenes series | People like seeing how things are made | :badge[Small]{tone="good"} | :progress{value="4" max="5"} |
| A community challenge | Brings people back every week | :badge[Medium]{tone="warn"} | :progress{value="3" max="5"} |
| A premium version | Funds the project | :badge[Large]{tone="bad"} | :progress{value="2" max="5"} |

## How an idea becomes a plan

:::steps
1. Write it down: One line in the table above, however rough.
2. Talk it through: When it comes up twice, give it its own page and an `idea` status.
3. Decide: Compare it with what's already planned, and log the call in the [[Decision log]].
4. Plan it: Add it to the [[Roadmap]] with a milestone.
:::

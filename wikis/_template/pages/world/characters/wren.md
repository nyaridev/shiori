Wren keeps the lanterns of the [[Old Grove]] burning and is the first person a new player meets. She sells the
[[Glow lantern]] and knows every path through the valley.

## Look

A long green coat, a lantern on a pole, and a fox that follows a few steps behind.

## Story

Wren came to Mossvale as a child, following a light that nobody else could see. She stayed to keep it lit, and
:spoiler[the light turns out to be her own lantern, from years in the future].

:::spoiler[What happens in the last chapter]
Wren hands her lantern to the player and walks into the grove. The lanterns stay lit.
:::

## In the game

- Teaches the first quest, *Light the path*
- Sells lanterns and oil at her stall

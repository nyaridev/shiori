A small flask of oil for the [[Glow lantern]]. One flask burns for five minutes.

## How to get it

1. [[Wren]] sells it at her stall, two coins a flask
2. Found in crates around the [[Old Grove]]

## Notes

- A lantern takes the next flask on its own when the last one runs out, as long as you carry one.
- Oil doesn't burn while the lantern is covered.[^covered]

## Tips

- Buy oil before dusk: Wren's stall closes when the [[Ember moth|ember moths]] come out.

A small lantern that burns [[Lantern oil]]. [[Wren]] sells them at her stall in the [[Old Grove]].

## Stats

| Stat | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Light radius | 12 | Studs[^radius] |
| Burn time | 5 min | Per oil |
| Weight | 1 | |

## How to get it

1. Every player starts with one
2. Buy another from [[Wren]] for 15 coins
3. A rare drop from the [[Moss fox]]

Refilling it costs :private[2 coins; we may raise it] a few coins.

## Notes

- A lit lantern keeps [[Ember moth|ember moths]] at a distance, but a swarm of five or more puts it out.[^swarm]
- [[Moss fox|Moss foxes]] follow anyone carrying one, lit or not. They're after the shine, not the light.
- The light goes out in water. Crossing the [[Willow River]] with it held up keeps it lit.

## Tips

- Cover the lantern (hold :kbd[F]) to walk past a swarm without losing it.
- Carry a spare [[Lantern oil]]: a lantern that runs dry at night can't be refilled until you reach a stall.

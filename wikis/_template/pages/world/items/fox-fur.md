Soft, green and faintly warm. [[Moss fox|Moss foxes]] drop it, and [[Wren]] trades for it at her stall.

## How to get it

1. Drops from a [[Moss fox]] six times in ten
2. Sometimes found snagged on brambles in the [[Old Grove]]

## Notes

- Fur picked up at night is worth the same as fur picked up by day, though players often say otherwise.

## Tips

- Moss foxes are easiest to find by day, asleep in the moss. Walk slowly and they won't wake.

The Old Grove is where players arrive and where [[Wren]] keeps her stall. Its paths are lit by lanterns at night.

## What's there

- Wren's lantern stall
- A hollow tree that hides a :spoiler[shortcut to the hills]
- Moss foxes by day and ember moths by night

## Layout

A ring of paths around a clearing, with the oldest tree in the middle.

:::private[Level design]
The hollow tree shortcut isn't built yet; it's blocked by a fallen log for now.
:::

## Notes

- The grove is the only place where lanterns are lit for you at night. Everywhere else, bring your own.
- Its paths run in a ring of about 400 studs, so a player who keeps walking ends up where they started.[^ring]

## Tips

- Lost? Follow the lit lanterns: every path that has them leads back to [[Wren]]'s stall.
- The clearing in the middle is the one place [[Ember moth|ember moths]] never swarm.

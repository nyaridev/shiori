The Willow River runs along the east side of the [[Old Grove]]. It's shallow enough to wade across, and nothing
from the grove follows you over it.

## Crossing

There's one ford, below the old willow. Wading takes about ten seconds; a [[Glow lantern]] held low goes out in the
water.[^ford]

## Notes

- Neither [[Moss fox|moss foxes]] nor [[Ember moth|ember moths]] cross the river, which makes the far bank the
  safest place in the valley at night.
- The water is shallow everywhere, but only the ford has solid ground; elsewhere players sink to their waist and
  slow down.

## Tips

- Hold your lantern up while you cross (keep :kbd[Space] pressed) to keep it lit.
- Coins a [[Moss fox]] stole sometimes wash up on the far bank.

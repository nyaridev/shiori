Ember moths swarm around any light after dark. One is harmless; a swarm can put out a lantern.

## Behaviour

They circle the brightest light nearby. Covering a [[Glow lantern]] makes them scatter.

## Notes

- Five or more moths around one lantern put it out.[^five] It can be lit again once they've gone.
- They never come out by day, and never fly over the [[Willow River]].
- A moth glows brighter the longer it circles a light, which makes a swarm easy to spot from the paths.

## Tips

- Cover your lantern before walking into the grove at night, and uncover it once you're past the swarm.
- A moth dies in one hit, but swinging at a swarm only draws more of them.

A mood board keeps everyone picturing the same thing. Collect screenshots, colors, thumbnails, packaging, scenes, or
anything that has the feeling we want, and say what we like about each.

## What we like

| Reference | What to borrow | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Dusk palette | Warm, calm colors that feel like evening | Too dark for small screens |
| Synthwave grid | Bold contrast, instantly recognizable | Overused; needs our own twist |
| Sketchbook UI | Personal, handmade feel | Can look unfinished |

:::callout[Adding references]{tone="tip"}
Put images in `assets/`, then add a line to the gallery: `![Name](/assets/file.png "What we like about it")`.
Videos and Figma boards play in place: `::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…"}`.
:::

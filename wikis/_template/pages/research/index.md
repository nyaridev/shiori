Research turns guesses into decisions. Every finding here links back to a source, so anyone can check it, and the
big ones end up in the [Decision log](/notes/decisions).

## Key findings

1. **People already look for this.** Searches and threads asking for it show up every week.
2. **The existing options are expensive or complicated.** That's the gap.
3. **Short videos drive discovery.** Most people in the [audience](audience) found similar things through clips.
